### A timeline, versions and undo on any Mac

- **Ticket:** S7V-032
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** The Editor now has a timeline under the player that shows every change the assistant makes. You can move, trim, split and delete clips by hand, or cut by deleting words in the transcript; every change can be undone and becomes a numbered version, an edit can use several clips, and none of it needs developer tools on your Mac.
- **Why:** You can fine-tune the assistant's first pass by hand, in a timeline that works like editors you already know.
