### iPhone photos open with the Mac's own decoder

- **Ticket:** S7V-014
- **Section:** Changed
- **What:** HEIC and HEIF photos you attach or preview are now decoded by macOS, the same way Preview opens them, and rotated iPhone photos show the right way up. In a plain browser, a HEIC photo asks you to convert it to JPEG first.
- **Why:** The previous decoder was LGPL code bundled inside the app, which our licence rules do not allow.
