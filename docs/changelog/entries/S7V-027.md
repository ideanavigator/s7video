### Remove silences by asking

- **Ticket:** S7V-027
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** Ask the agent to remove the silences and it cuts the pauses between your sentences without clipping words, keeps any pause you ask for, and tells you how much it removed. Undo brings the previous edit back.
- **Why:** Cutting dead air is the slowest part of a first pass on talking-head footage.
