### Edit your video by editing its words

- **Ticket:** S7V-038
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** Select words in the transcript and delete them to cut that part of the video, or restore struck-through words to bring them back. The timeline and player follow, and undo works.
- **Why:** Cutting by text is the fastest way to tighten a talk, and it shares one edit with the timeline and the agent.
