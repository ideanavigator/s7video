### Outside changes to your edit are checked

- **Ticket:** S7V-054
- **Section:** Security
- **What:** If an assistant changes your edit without the app's video tools, the change is kept only if it is valid and adds no file from outside your project; otherwise your edit goes back to its last saved version and you see why.
- **Why:** Edits made behind the app's back could break the project or pull in files from anywhere on your Mac.
