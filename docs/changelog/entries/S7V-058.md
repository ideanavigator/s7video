### Tighter cuts after a first cut, and waiting for transcription

- **Ticket:** S7V-058
- **Section:** Fixed
- **What:** Asking for tighter cuts on a clip whose silences were already removed now cuts more instead of failing, and never brings back an earlier cut. When you ask to remove silences on a new clip, the agent waits for the words to be found instead of stopping halfway, and the job shows when the speech model is loading and keeps moving while words are timed.
- **Why:** A second pass failed every time, and on a first transcription the agent gave up and you had to send another message.
