### Exports only when you ask, in the clip's own shape

- **Ticket:** S7V-061
- **Section:** Fixed
- **What:** The assistant no longer makes a video after every edit, and a wide clip exports wide unless you ask for a reel or a square. You can ask for 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3, and the player shows that shape inside its frame, with bars around it.
- **Why:** Editing filled the exports folder with copies, and wide clips came out as cropped reels.

### Ask for a video shape

- **Ticket:** S7V-061
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** Ask the assistant for a reel (9:16), wide (16:9), square (1:1), 4:5 or 4:3 video, and it changes the edit's shape in one step that undo takes back. The player and the export both use that shape.
- **Why:** A new edit takes its first clip's shape, and the user needs a way to ask for another one.
