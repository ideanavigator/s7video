### Plan mode only looks; files come in only through Assets or chat

- **Ticket:** S7V-066
- **Section:** Fixed
- **What:** A chat in plan mode can still look at your video, read its transcript and check on jobs, but no longer imports, transcribes, cuts silences, exports, undoes or redoes; it tells you to switch out of plan mode first. A connected device that may change your edit can no longer add a file from elsewhere on your Mac to a project and then play or download it; files come in only through Assets or chat, which need permission to read your files.
- **Why:** Plan mode is meant to be safe to try ideas in, and edit permission should not quietly grant access to every file on your Mac.
