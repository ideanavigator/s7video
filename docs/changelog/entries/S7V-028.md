### Export with captions

- **Ticket:** S7V-028
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** Export your edit as an MP4 with captions burned in, at −14 LUFS, using your Mac's video hardware. The export reports its progress, you can cancel it, and the finished video lands in the project's `exports` folder.
- **Why:** A creator should go from raw take to a postable reel without another app.
