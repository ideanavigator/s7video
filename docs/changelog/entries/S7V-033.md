### Timeline under the player

- **Ticket:** S7V-033
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** The Editor panel now shows a CapCut-style timeline with video, caption, sound effect, music and overlay rows. It follows playback, jumps where you click, and zooms around the pointer, and it stays smooth with a thousand cuts.
