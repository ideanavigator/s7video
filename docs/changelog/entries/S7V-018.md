### Remove SSH, Tailscale and pull-request screens

- **Ticket:** S7V-018
- **Section:** Removed
- **What:** No options for connecting to other computers over SSH, Tailscale or WSL. The "Create PR" action and the pull-request settings are gone, and the pull-request screens are hidden. Commit and push still work, and other computers on your network can still pair with your Mac.
- **Why:** s7video edits video on one Mac, so Settings only shows what applies to that.
