### Transcription on your Mac

- **Ticket:** S7V-026
- **Section:** Added
- **What:** s7video transcribes your clips word by word on your own Mac. It asks once before downloading the speech model (about 3.7 GB, or 2.3 GB for the light model), then works offline, and each clip is transcribed only once.
- **Why:** Silence cuts, captions and the transcript view all need exact word timings, and your footage should never leave your Mac.
