### Download the speech model from setup or the Editor panel

- **Ticket:** S7V-067
- **Section:** Fixed
- **What:** While the speech model isn't downloaded, the Editor panel says so at the top with a Download speech model button, and the panel opens by itself when the assistant needs the model, so you no longer have to find the Transcript tab. First-run setup and Settings → Providers show a Speech model item beside your agents: pick Standard or Light (Light is recommended on Macs with 8 GB of memory or less), then download it with progress and cancel, or skip it for later. If the same model is already in a Hugging Face cache on your Mac, it says "Found on this Mac, ready to use" and Use takes it without downloading, after checking every file. The assistant now names the button, and no longer worries that a clip must be on a track before cutting silences.
- **Why:** On a fresh install the assistant asked people to accept a download that nothing in the panel offered, and they were stuck.
