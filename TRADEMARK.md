# Trademark policy

The s7video source code is MIT-licensed: you may use, copy, modify and sell it. The MIT licence covers
copyright only. It does **not** grant rights to the **s7video** name, the s7video logo or icons, or
anything that suggests your product is s7video or is endorsed by the s7video project. This policy
explains what you may do with those marks.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product is "based on s7video" or "a fork of s7video", or that it works
  with s7video.
- Using the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and package
  listings of unmodified builds.
- Distributing unmodified official builds or unmodified source under the s7video name.
- Linking to the project.

## Forks must rebrand

If you distribute a modified version, whether free or paid, you must:

- give it a different name and logo that will not be confused with s7video;
- change the app name, bundle ID, URL scheme, icons and any user-facing "s7video" text; all of these
  are set in one brand configuration file, so this is a small change;
- keep the copyright and licence notices required by the MIT licence, including those of T3 Code;
- not imply that the s7video project made, reviewed or endorses your version.

## Not allowed

- Using "s7video" or a confusingly similar name in the name of another product, company or domain.
- Using the s7video logo or icons in a modified build or in your own marketing.
- Selling a modified build as "s7video".
